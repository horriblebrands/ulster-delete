Rugs

Custom Axminster Rugs crafted for hospitality.

Shaped to
your space

Every bespoke rug begins in the same way as our custom Axminster carpets. Developed in collaboration with our design team, each rug is created to suit the individual project before being expertly cut and finished to your chosen specification. The result is a truly bespoke piece that complements the wider interior scheme while delivering exceptional quality and craftsmanship.

Designed for hospitality, commercial and residential environments, our rugs bring warmth, comfort and visual impact to a wide range of spaces. From hotel guestrooms and suites to lounges, reception areas, meeting spaces and private residences, each rug can be tailored to suit its setting.

Every rug is made to order and can be produced in custom dimensions, shapes and layouts. Whether you require a statement oversized feature rug, a circular design, a bespoke border detail or a unique shape to complement the space, our team can help create a solution that fits perfectly.

Each rug is completed by skilled craftspeople who ensure every piece is as beautifully finished as it is designed.

The finishing details are what bring a rug to life. From hand-finished borders to a variety of edge treatments, each rug is completed by skilled craftspeople who ensure every piece is as beautifully finished as it is designed. In addition to bespoke designs, many of our stocked carpet collections can also be transformed into custom rugs. This offers a simple and flexible way to introduce coordinating designs into a scheme, with the same range of finishing options available to create a polished, tailored result.
