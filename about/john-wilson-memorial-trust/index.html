The John Wilson Memorial Trust

The John Wilson Memorial Trust 

The John Wilson Memorial Trust (JWMT) was set up in memory of the late John Wilson, son of Ulster Carpets founder, George Walter Wilson, following his passing in 2009.

John Wilson always had an enduring passion for business, particularly the long held establishment of significant employment in Portadown that is in keeping with Ulster Carpets' place in the community.

The Trust is run by nominated Trustees involved in Ulster Carpets, including two of John's sons, and continues the company's commitment to supporting the local community.

Supporting Our People

Through the John Wilson Trust, employees at Ulster Carpets have access to three support initiatives designed to give back to both individuals and communities.

Champion A Cause

Champion a Cause gives employees the opportunity to secure funding from the John Wilson Trust for a local charity of their choice, making a positive difference in their communities.

Thrive

Thrive offers wellbeing funding for employees to take part in physical, creative, or learning activities outside of work, supporting a healthier and more balanced lifestyle.

Impact Explorers

Impact Explorers offers travel bursaries to employees and their families, supporting participation in overseas charitable and volunteer projects.
