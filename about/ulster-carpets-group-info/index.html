The Ulster Group

The Ulster Group is a collection of complementary companies delivering the highest standards of innovation and quality in flooring across the world.

Roger Oates Design

Luxury interiors brand, Roger Oates Design, combine artisan heritage with a contemporary approach to design and make luxury 100% wool rugs and runners.

VISIT

Danfloor

Danish company, Danfloor, are specialist manufacturers and suppliers of commercial carpets for market sectors including Healthcare, Leisure and Education.

VISIT

Alternative Flooring

British flooring brand, Alternative Flooring, design and manufacture high-quality, patterned carpets, rugs, and runners, focusing on natural fibres like wool, sisal, coir, and seagrass, along with faux materials.

VISIT

Solidwool

Sustainable furniture and materials producer, Solidwool, have created a new way of working with wool to create a unique composite material.

VISIT

Axfelt

Based in Devon, Axfelt manufacture luxury underlay for home and hospitality projects made from recycled materials including recycled wool fibres, yarn and wool-rich carpet waste.

GTM

Technical textile machinery manufacturer, GTM, manufacture innovative solutions for 3D weaving, composites and non-woven applications.

VISIT

Ulster Yarns

Located in Yorkshire, Ulster Yarns have been producing premium woollen yarn since the industrial revolution. This expert wool processing and dyeing company operates across two sites in Dewsbury and Wakefield.
