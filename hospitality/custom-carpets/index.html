Custom Axminster carpets gives interior designers the freedom to create with confidence, combining exceptional design flexibility with proven long-term performance. From subtle textures to intricate patterns, bespoke carpets can be developed to reflect the unique character of any project while meeting the demands of commercial hospitality environments.

With unlimited creative possibilities in pattern, colour and scale, Axminster can be tailored to suit every space. Whether designing for guestrooms, corridors, public areas or lounges, each carpet can be adapted to meet both the aesthetic vision and functional requirements of the environment.

Manufactured in Northern Ireland, every custom Axminster carpet is backed by generations of craftsmanship and technical expertise.

Available in a range of constructions, row counts and pile heights, our custom Axminster carpets can be specified to deliver the right balance of comfort, durability and appearance retention. Options include both 100% wool and 80/20 wool-rich blends, ensuring the most appropriate solution for every application. For more technical guidance, explore our Resources section. From concept development and colour consultation through to yarn spinning, dyeing and weaving, our team provides expert support at every stage.
