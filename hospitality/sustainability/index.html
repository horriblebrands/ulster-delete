Community

Supporting people has always been part of the Ulster Carpets story.

As a fourth generation family business, the Wilson family continue to lead and shape Ulster Carpets today, carrying forward the values the company was founded on in 1938.

Through community initiatives, education partnerships and employee-led support programmes, we continue to invest in the communities around us. We are incredibly proud of the legacy built by the Wilson family and believe it is important to continue that commitment through meaningful, long term support for local people, future generations, and causes connected to our employees and communities.

That is why initiatives such as the John Wilson Memorial Trust and the Walter Wilson Scholarship remain such an important part of Ulster Carpets today, helping us continue to support communities, education and opportunity for future generations.
