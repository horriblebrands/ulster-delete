With a passion for design and a drive for innovation, we manufacture luxury carpets for homes and hospitality projects across the world.

A Proud
History

 

Combining time honoured techniques with patented weaving innovation, Ulster Carpets have grown to become a world-renowned manufacturer of luxury carpets and rugs for the hospitality and home sectors.

From custom designed carpets for the world’s most luxurious hotels, casinos, and cruise ships to distinctively designed carpets and rugs for your home, Ulster Carpets create flooring that resonates.

The Ulster Group

From humble beginnings in Northern Ireland, we have grown into a global group of companies that complement our ethos of luxury, quality, and sustainable flooring solutions.
