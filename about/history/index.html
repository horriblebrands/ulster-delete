An enduring legacy

The integrity, craftsmanship, and the deep sense of responsibility to our community set by our company founder remain at the core of our company

Our Story

For nearly 90 years, Ulster Carpets have been crafting woven carpets that combine heritage, creativity and enduring quality.

As a family-owned business, we have grown from our roots in Northern Ireland to become a trusted partner for designers, architects and hospitality brands around the world. While our reach has expanded globally, our values remain firmly grounded in the people, skills and craftsmanship that have defined us since 1938.

At the heart of our business are generations of talented designers, weavers and makers whose expertise transforms yarn into beautiful, highly crafted interiors. Many of the skills used within our manufacturing process have been refined over decades, combining traditional weaving knowledge with contemporary innovation and design.

From luxurious hotel suites and elegant public spaces to beautifully designed homes, our carpets are created to enrich interiors with warmth, comfort and character.

Whether welcoming guests, creating memorable experiences or enhancing everyday living, every Ulster carpet is thoughtfully designed to perform beautifully while standing the test of time. Today, our carpets can be found in some of the world's leading hotels, resorts, cruise ships, casinos, commercial environments and private residences. Yet every project, regardless of scale, is guided by the same principles - exceptional craftsmanship, thoughtful design and a commitment to lasting quality.

Inspired by
the Future

Proud of our heritage and inspired by the future, we continue to invest in our people, our skills and our craft, ensuring that every carpet carries forward the legacy of generations while meeting the demands of contemporary interiors.

From family homes to the world's finest hospitality destinations, every Ulster carpet is woven with the same commitment to craftsmanship, quality and enduring design.
