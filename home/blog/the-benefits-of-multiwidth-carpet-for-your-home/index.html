The Benefits of Multiwidth Carpet for Your Home

When choosing the perfect carpet for your home, colour, texture and design are often at the top of the list. However, there's another important factor worth considering and that is carpet width. At Ulster Carpets, our MultiWidths service is designed to help you make the most of your chosen carpet, offering greater flexibility, better value and less waste, without compromising on quality or style. Whether you're updating your living room, hallway, stairs or landing, here's why choosing MultiWidths could make all the difference.

Better Value for Your Home

When purchasing a new carpet, it's important to consider the final installed cost rather than simply the price per square metre.

Our MultiWidths service allows you to select the most suitable carpet widths for your space, helping to reduce the amount of excess carpet required. This can be particularly beneficial for hallways, stairs and landings, where using a combination of widths can help lower overall installation costs.

The result? Beautiful, high-quality carpet with better value for your home. 

Less Waste, More Efficiency

Choosing the right carpet width isn't just good for your budget – it can also help reduce waste.

On a typical hallway, stairs and landing installation, our MultiWidths service can reduce the amount of carpet required by up to 20%.

By making more efficient use of materials, MultiWidths helps minimise unnecessary offcuts while ensuring you still achieve the beautiful finish you expect from Ulster Carpets. 

More Widths, More Flexibility

Every home is different, which is why having a choice of carpet widths can make such a difference.

At Ulster Carpets, we offer a range of widths across our collections:

• Axminster carpets: 4.57m, 3.66m, 2.74m, 1.83m and 0.91m.

• Wilton and Tufted carpets: 5m, 4m, 3m, 2m and 1m.

This flexibility allows your carpet fitter to choose the most appropriate widths for each area of your home, helping to minimise joins and create a seamless, professional finish.

Consistent Colour Throughout Your Home

Creating a cohesive look throughout your home is easy with MultiWidths.

Our different carpet widths are carefully colour-matched, allowing you to carry your chosen design from room to room with confidence.

Whether you're coordinating your stairs and landing or creating a consistent look across multiple spaces, MultiWidths helps achieve a beautifully connected interior.

For the best colour consistency, we recommend ordering all required widths together.

A Smarter Way to Carpet Your Home

At Ulster Carpets, we believe beautiful flooring should combine quality, practicality and thoughtful design.

Our MultiWidths service offers a smarter way to carpet your home, helping you achieve a high-quality finish while reducing waste and making the most of your budget.
