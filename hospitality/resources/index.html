FAQs

Answers to all your carpet questions. If you can't find the answer here, please get in touch with us and we will be happy to help.

  • Every project begins with a conversation. Whether you have a completed design, a mood board, architectural drawings, or simply an idea, our team will help guide you through the next steps.

    From the earliest concepts, we work closely with customers to develop designs, colour palettes, layouts, and specifications that are tailored specifically to your project. Whatever stage you're at, contact us and we'll help turn your vision into a beautiful woven carpet.

  • Every project places different demands on a carpet. Guestrooms, corridors, ballrooms and public spaces all experience different levels of traffic and require different performance characteristics. Our team works closely with designers and specifiers to recommend the most appropriate construction, material and specification for each environment, balancing creative intent with long-term performance. We have some useful guides in the Specifying Carpet resources - CLICK HERE

  • Axminster is a woven carpet construction that combines exceptional creative freedom with long-term performance. Individually dyed yarns are woven together to create intricate patterns with remarkable clarity, allowing designers to explore everything from subtle textures and tonal gradients to highly detailed artwork.

    Available in both 100% wool and 80/20 wool/nylon carpet pile blends, Axminster remains the preferred choice for many hospitality interiors around the world.

    Read our Understanding Axminster brochure - CLICK HERE

  • Uniweave is our bespoke design and planning approach, where every carpet is created specifically for the space. Unlike a traditional repeating pattern, Uniweave uses a non-repeating design that responds to the unique layout, architecture, and function of each individual area.

    Our Designers and Planners work together from the earliest stages of every project, balancing creativity with technical planning to ensure the finished installation reflects the original design vision.

  • Yes. Our Design team regularly develops bespoke colour palettes inspired by fabrics, stone, timber, artwork and other interior finishes. Working closely with customers, we create colours that complement the wider scheme while making the most of what woven carpet can achieve.
