Neutrals Never Fail

There’s a reason why neutrals never fail. You can easily coordinate them with other colours, add a splash of colour, and introduce other textures and styles.

While some consider them the safe choice, those who are serious about their interior design see neutrals as the strong foundation to build everything else around.

The 10 new colour additions to our timeless Grange Wilton range are rooted in a renewed connection to nature and the everyday rituals that shape more mindful, wholesome living.

CREATE CALMNESS

Drawing inspiration from the landscapes around us, the small, intentional choices we make with the food we enjoy, and the spaces we curate, these shades reflect a calmer, more grounded way of life.

Soft, organic tones echo natural materials and evolving environments, creating interiors that feel nurturing, balanced and effortlessly timeless. Designed to resonate with modern consumers who value authenticity and wellbeing, the palette brings a quiet warmth and considered elegance to the home, allowing nature’s influence to be felt in a refined, contemporary way.

LIVING WELL PHILOSOPHY

This range embraces the ‘Living Well’ philosophy appealing to conscious consumers who thoughtfully choose to create the lifestyle they value.

The introduction of warm, nature-inspired tones with subtle hints of gold, green, and brown enhances this natural look, while preserving the practical benefits and comfort of pure wool.

With over 40 colours available and an Extra Heavy Wear suitability rating, the Grange Wilton range is suitable for any room in the home.

Explore the complete collection in our Choose a Carpet section where you can also use our Visualiser tool to view the carpet in your room.
