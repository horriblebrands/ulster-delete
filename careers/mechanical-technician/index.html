Mechanical Technician

We are seeking applications for a Mechanical Technician to work with the mechanical systems and production equipment within our operations facility.

Key Duties will involve:

  • The set-up, maintenance, repair and implementation of mechanical solutions that addresses root cause in relation to production machinery and equipment, ensuring maximum efficiency and quality.  

  • Recognising faults and the correction of faults associated with production machinery and equipment.

  • Apply mechanical and technical knowledge to production processes to support improvements and reduce recurring issues

 

Candidates must be able to demonstrate the following criteria:

 

Essential Criteria:

  • Be able to demonstrate hand-on technical experience

  • Experience in a previous role in the set-up and maintenance of industrial equipment

  • Prepared to work shifts and overtime as required

  • IT Literate

  • Proactive attitude

  • A range of interpersonal skills to include good communication skills, organisational skills and the ability to work on own initiative or as part of a team

 

Desirable Criteria:

  • A qualification in Technical/Mechanical Maintenance or similar/related discipline

  • Experience in pneumatics

  • Basic electrical knowledge

  • Welding/milling/turning experience

 

Rate of pay will be dependent on skills and experience on commencing.

 

The Company is committed to selection on merit
