Wool Felt Underlay

Our 100% recyclable wool felt underlay made from wool-rich textile waste.

EXPLORE WOOL FELT

100% recyclable
underlay

Wool Felt is a high-quality felt underlay manufactured using wool-rich yarn and carpet waste that has been diverted from landfill. Designed for General Wear in commercial and residential applications, at the end of its life, Wool Felt can be returned to our facility in Devon where it is recycled into a component part of Axfelt Triple Layer Underlay.

Axfelt Triple
Layer Underlay

Axfelt Triple Layer Underlay is a premium underlay designed primarily for residential interiors, with selected commercial applications. Manufactured in the UK using more than 82% recycled materials, its unique three-layer construction delivers lasting comfort, durability and support while helping to extend the life of your carpet. Backed by a 10-year guarantee, it's a sustainable choice without compromising on performance.
