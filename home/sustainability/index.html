Sustainability 

An insight into how materials make a difference.

Sustainable by nature

Wool is naturally carbon sequestering, meaning it stores carbon within the fibre itself.

Combined with its natural resilience, comfort and long-lasting performance, these qualities make wool an exceptional choice for the home. Its unique fibre structure allows wool to recover from compression, helping carpets retain their appearance through everyday life. Wool also naturally absorbs sound, helping create quieter interiors, while its insulating properties provide warmth and comfort underfoot throughout the seasons. The fibre's natural protective outer layer helps resist soiling, making carpets easier to maintain, while its ability to absorb and release moisture contributes to a comfortable indoor environment. Naturally flame resistant and antistatic, wool offers a combination of practical performance and everyday comfort that has made it the material of choice for generations.
