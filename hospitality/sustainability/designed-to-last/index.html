Design to Last

At Ulster, we respect the skill, craftsmanship, materials and energy that go into every carpet we create. That is why designing products to reach their full longevity potential has always been an important part of how we work.

Design to Last

Every year that a carpet remains in use is another year the materials and energy invested in its creation continue to deliver value. Ensuring our products perform beautifully for as long as possible is one of the most meaningful ways we can reduce their environmental impact and an important part of our responsibility.

Designing for Performance

Designing for longevity begins long before a carpet is manufactured. It starts with understanding how a space will be used, selecting the right construction and materials for the environment, and creating designs that continue to look and perform well over time.

The Right Material for the Right Space

Hospitality interiors place unique demands on flooring. Materials must withstand constant use while continuing to support the creative vision of the space, maintaining comfort, acoustics and appearance throughout years of service. Wool has remained central to woven hospitality carpets because it brings these qualities together naturally, allowing products to continue performing in demanding environments for longer.

Our custom Axminster carpets are available in both 100% wool and an 80/20 wool/nylon blend, each offering different performance characteristics. Selecting the right material and specification for the intended environment is an important part of designing for longevity, ensuring every carpet is developed to perform as intended throughout its life.

Axminster Construction

The woven construction of Axminster naturally supports this approach to longevity. Its dimensional stability, durable woven structure and exceptional design definition allow Axminster carpets to perform in demanding hospitality environments while maintaining the clarity of the original design. Combined with a choice of different specifications, each carpet can be developed to suit the specific demands of its intended environment, from guestrooms to busy public spaces. Designing the right carpet for the right application helps ensure materials are used thoughtfully while supporting long-term performance.

Appearance Retention

Long-term performance is influenced by more than material selection alone. Thoughtful use of colour, pattern and construction helps carpets retain their appearance over time, ensuring they continue to support the character of a space through years of everyday use. Designing to last is about making informed decisions from the very beginning. By combining appropriate materials, thoughtful design and woven construction, we create products that remain valuable for longer, reducing the need for premature replacement while continuing to support exceptional hospitality interiors.
