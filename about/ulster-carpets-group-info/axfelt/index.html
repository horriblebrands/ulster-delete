Axfelt

Based in Devon, Axfelt recycles waste materials and transforms them into luxury underlay for both home and hospitality projects.

Axfelt is at the heart of our Make with Purpose ethos.

As the Recycling Hub for the Ulster Group, Axfelt transforms waste materials created during our manufacturing processes into two different underlays – Axfelt Triple Layer and our 100% recyclable underlay, Wool Felt.
