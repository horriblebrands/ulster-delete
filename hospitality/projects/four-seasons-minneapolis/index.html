Four Seasons Hotel

Minneapolis

Design Firm

Smallwood, Reynolds, Stewart, Stewart & Assoc

Photography

by Don Riddle, courtesy of Four Seasons Minneapolis

View Gallery

Four Seasons Minneapolis and Ulster Carpets proved to be the perfect partnership as we designed and manufactured a wide range of carpets and rugs for this beautiful hotel.

Located just two blocks from the Mississippi and close to North Loop shopping and entertainment in downtown Minneapolis, the Four Seasons toasts the city’s history while setting the stage for the future.

Ulster Carpets understand the importance of respecting the past while also building towards the future. That was evidenced by the high standards of customer service, that the Ulster brand has always been renowned for, coupled with the latest in design and weaving expertise.

The custom designs of our carpets and rugs throughout the Four Seasons Minneapolis creates a sense of continuity while giving each area of the hotel its own sense identity.

Understated Elegance

That is clear when you compare the designs in the ballroom, boardroom and meeting rooms where a linear style dominates with the understated elegance of the carpets and rugs in the public areas, such as the pre-function lounge and lobby.

The custom rugs in the Spa and the Presidential Suite follow similar themes, adding to the sense of sophistication that guests associate with the Four Seasons brand.

That the various designs work in harmony is testament to the design skills at Ulster Carpets. Working in collaboration with the talented team at Smallwood, Reynolds, Stewart, Stewart & Assoc, our designers were able to ensure that the colourways and patterns on the carpets and rugs complemented the overall interior design style of the hotel.

By blending together these various elements in a subtle yet visually engaging way, the carpets and rugs create a firm foundation to build the entire interior design around.
