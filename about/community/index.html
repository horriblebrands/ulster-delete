Supporting Our Community

Community has always been at the heart of Ulster Carpets.

For nearly 90 years, we have believed in supporting the places and people around us, recognising that lasting businesses help create lasting communities. Today, we continue that commitment through a range of local initiatives, including The John Wilson Memorial Trust and the Walter Wilson Scholarship. Together, they reflect our ongoing investment in local organisations, education and future generations. Discover more below.
