Axfelt Triple Layer Underlay 

Three layers of comfort, warmth and durability

3 Layers of Quality

Delivering three layers of comfort, warmth and durability, Axfelt Triple Layer Underlay is made from textile waste diverted from landfill and recycled, remade and renewed into the perfect foundation for your home.

With a 10-year guarantee when installed with a woven Ulster carpets (Ts&Cs apply), the three-layer construction can help to significantly extend the life of your new carpet.

Made from recycled materials, Axfelt Triple Layer Underlay offers a more sustainable foundation for your new carpet without compromising on performance. Its unique three-layer construction delivers outstanding comfort, durability, and resilience, providing exceptional crush recovery to help your carpet maintain its appearance for longer.

The natural insulating properties of wool, combined with the innovative layered design, improve both thermal efficiency and sound reduction, creating a warmer, quieter home. Designed to work particularly well with woven carpets, the felt surface helps secure the carpet in place, reduces movement over time, and allows seams to settle more naturally for a smoother finish.

Contract rated for long-lasting performance, Axfelt provides reliable support that helps extend the life of your carpet, making it a practical, comfortable, and environmentally responsible choice.
